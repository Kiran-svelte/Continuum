Security & Trust Page 8
Honest expectations

What Continuum is — and isn't

We'd rather set expectations upfront than oversell.

✓ Continuum IS

  • A complete HRMS for Indian SMBs — leave through payroll
  • Config-driven — policies and approvals without code deploys
  • Multi-tenant SaaS with per-company module control
  • Role-based portals for admin, HR, manager, employee
  • Audit-ready with statutory export paths (PF, reports)
  • Invite-only — your admin controls who joins
  • Web-first, mobile-responsive (not a native app)

✗ Continuum IS NOT

  • A generic project management or CRM tool
  • A replacement for your chartered accountant (advisory)
  • Biometric hardware — we integrate attendance data, not turnstiles
  • Guaranteed legal advice — compliance tools, not a law firm
  • Instant migration from every legacy HR system
  • A public signup free-for-all — companies are provisioned
  • Offline-first — core workflows need connectivity
